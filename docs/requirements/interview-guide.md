# Interview guide — finding a project supervisor

**Method:** short semi-structured interviews (~10 min) with final-year / soon-to-be
final-year students. Aim for **5–6 participants**. Record answers verbatim where possible
and note anything distinctive (frustration, strong preferences).

**Purpose (read to participant):** "I'm designing an app that helps students find a
final-year project supervisor. There are no right or wrong answers — I just want to
understand how you'd want this to work. You can stop at any time and your answers are
anonymous."

## Screening
1. Are you in your final year, or about to be? (If not, they can still answer hypothetically.)

## Current process & pain points
2. Have you thought about who might supervise your final-year project?
3. How do you currently find out which staff supervise which topics?
4. What's frustrating or unclear about that process right now?

## What matters when choosing
5. When choosing a supervisor, what matters most to you? *(probe: research areas,
   specific project ideas, availability/capacity, approachability, past projects)*
6. Do you care more about a supervisor's broad **areas of interest**, or their specific
   **proposed project ideas**? Why?
7. Would knowing how many students a supervisor can still take change your decision?

## Desired features
8. Would you want to **search or filter** staff by topic? Which topics matter to you?
9. How would you want to **approach or contact** a potential supervisor?
10. On first opening such an app, what would you want to see?
11. Any features you'd love? *(probe: bookmarking/shortlist, recommendations, deadlines)*

## Wrap-up
12. Anything else about finding a supervisor that we haven't covered?

---
### Analysis notes (fill in after interviews)
For each interview, tag answers with candidate themes, then count theme frequency
across participants to prioritise requirements (mandatory / desirable / possible).

Candidate themes: *discoverability & search · areas vs. project ideas ·
contacting / requesting supervision · capacity & availability · personalisation /
shortlisting · trust & fit.*
